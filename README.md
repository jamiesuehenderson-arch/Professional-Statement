# PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT
## CYBERSECURITY | RISK MANAGEMENT | INFORMATION SECURITY

I am a dedicated, results-driven professional transitioning into the cybersecurity industry with 19 years of experience in business management, strategic leadership, operations, and organizational development. My professional background has equipped me with strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, risk assessment, communication, and decision-making skills. I hold a PhD in Business Management with a specialization in Strategy and Innovation, and I am committed to expanding my technical knowledge to build a successful career in cybersecurity.

My core values include **integrity, accountability, ethical responsibility, confidentiality, continuous improvement, and protecting people and organizations**. I am passionate about understanding emerging cyber threats, safeguarding sensitive information, and helping organizations develop effective security strategies that support their business objectives.

### How My Strengths, Values, and Interests Support Organizational Cybersecurity Goals

**1. Strategic Risk Management**

With 19 years of business management experience, I understand the importance of identifying potential risks, evaluating their impact, and implementing strategies to protect organizational assets. I aim to apply my business knowledge alongside cybersecurity principles to support risk assessments, vulnerability management, regulatory compliance, and effective security decision-making.

**2. Proactive Threat Identification and Prevention**

My analytical thinking, attention to detail, and problem-solving abilities provide a strong foundation for identifying potential security vulnerabilities. I am committed to developing the technical expertise needed to recognize cyber threats, evaluate security weaknesses, and contribute to preventive measures that reduce organizational exposure to cyberattacks.

**3. Leadership and Security Awareness**

My extensive leadership experience has strengthened my ability to train employees, communicate expectations, develop policies, and promote accountability. I can apply these strengths to cybersecurity awareness initiatives, helping employees understand security best practices, recognize potential threats, and contribute to a security-conscious workplace culture.

**4. Commitment to Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability**

I recognize the importance of the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA) triad in protecting information systems. I value safeguarding sensitive information, maintaining accurate and reliable data, and supporting the availability of essential business systems. These principles align with my commitment to ethical responsibility and organizational trust.

**5. Continuous Learning and Professional Development**

I strongly believe that professional growth requires adaptability, discipline, and a commitment to lifelong learning. As I pursue cybersecurity education and industry-recognized certifications, I am developing knowledge in network security, security frameworks, threat analysis, security controls, and incident response. My goal is to continuously strengthen my technical capabilities to address evolving cybersecurity challenges.

**6. Security Operations and Incident Response**

My experience managing business operations, resolving complex problems, and making decisions under pressure has prepared me to work effectively in demanding environments. I am interested in developing practical skills in security monitoring, incident investigation, threat detection, and response procedures to help organizations minimize disruptions and protect critical systems.

**7. Compliance, Ethics, and Professional Accountability**

Throughout my management career, I have emphasized compliance, operational standards, confidentiality, and accountability. I understand that cybersecurity professionals have a responsibility to protect information, respect privacy, and follow applicable laws and organizational policies. I am committed to applying these values to support ethical security practices and responsible information management.

**8. Collaboration and Effective Communication**

I believe successful cybersecurity requires collaboration between technical teams, business leaders, and employees. My background in leadership and organizational management allows me to communicate complex information clearly, coordinate teams, and support the implementation of security initiatives that align with broader business objectives.

### My Professional Career Objective

My goal is to successfully transition into cybersecurity, beginning with opportunities in information security, security operations, or governance, risk, and compliance (GRC). I intend to combine my 19 years of business leadership experience with developing technical expertise to help organizations identify vulnerabilities, manage security risks, protect digital assets, and strengthen their overall security posture.

I am committed to becoming a knowledgeable, ethical, and dependable cybersecurity professional who brings both strategic business insight and technical security capabilities to the organizations I serve.
